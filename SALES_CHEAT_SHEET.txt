WHAT IS KERJA MUDAH?
--------------------
Kerja Mudah is a simple business management app designed specifically for small shops in Malaysia. It runs on your Windows computer - no internet needed, no monthly fees. You manage jobs, customers, invoices, and appointments all in one place.

Think of it as your digital job card system, but smarter. It remembers everything so you don't have to.

Perfect for:
- Phone repair shops

- Electronics repair shops
- Any service-based business

A: That's exactly why we made it simple. If you can send a WhatsApp message, you can use Kerja Mudah. The setup takes 1 minute - just enter your business name and phone number. No complicated menus, no confusing options. Everything is laid out clearly.
