# Kerja Mudah - Business Management App

## What Is Kerja Mudah?

Kerja Mudah (which means "Easy Work" in Malay) is a desktop application designed specifically for repair shops and service businesses in Malaysia. It helps you manage your daily operations — from tracking repairs to sending invoices — all in one place.

---

## The Problem

Running a repair shop means juggling multiple things at once:

- **Lost track of jobs** — Where is that phone? Is it repaired? Who's it for?
- **Missed payments** — Did that customer pay? How much is outstanding?
- **No-show customers** — They never picked up their device. How do you remind them?
- **Manual paperwork** — Writing invoices by hand, tracking everything in notebooks
- **Language barriers** — Your team speaks different languages, but your tools only support one

---

## What Kerja Mudah Solves

### 1. Track Every Job
- Create a job for every repair with a unique code (e.g., JOB-20260825-1)
- Track status: Pending → In Progress → Done
- Link each job to a customer
- Add notes about the problem, parts needed, or special instructions

### 2. Manage Your Customers
- Store customer name, phone, email, and notes
- See all jobs for any customer with one click
- Search anything instantly — name, phone, job code, item

### 3. Send Professional Invoices
- Generate PDF invoices automatically from completed jobs
- Send via WhatsApp or Email with one click
- Track paid and unpaid invoices
- Include your business details and Google review link

### 4. Get Paid Faster
- Track outstanding payments
- See today's, weekly, and monthly revenue
- Know exactly who owes you money

### 5. Never Lose a Customer
- Track uncollected devices
- Send automatic WhatsApp reminders to customers
- "Hi Ahmad, your iPhone repair is ready for pickup!"

### 6. Schedule Appointments
- Book appointments with date, time, and purpose
- Add notes about what the customer needs
- See all upcoming appointments

### 7. Get More Google Reviews
- Add your Google review link in Settings
- It's automatically included when you send invoices
- More reviews = more customers find you

### 8. Works in Your Language
- Switch between English, Bahasa Malaysia, and Chinese
- All your team members can use it comfortably

## How It Works

### Step 1: Add a Customer
Enter the customer's name, phone number, and email. You can add notes about their preferences or history.

### Step 2: Create a Job
When a customer brings in a device:
- Select the customer
- Describe the item and problem
- Set a quote (price)
- Set a due date
- Add any notes

The app generates a unique job code automatically.

### Step 3: Update the Job
As you work on the repair:
- Change status from Pending to In Progress
- Add notes about what you found
- Update the quote if needed

### Step 4: Complete the Job
When the repair is done:
- Mark the job as Done
- Generate an invoice
- Send it via WhatsApp or Email

### Step 5: Get Paid
- Mark the invoice as Paid when you receive payment
- Track all payments in the Reports section
