========================================
  KERJA MUDAH - USER GUIDE
========================================

Thank you for purchasing Kerja Mudah!

QUICK START
-----------
1. Double-click "Kerja Mudah.exe" to run the app
2. The Setup Wizard will guide you through:
   - Enter your business name
   - Enter your phone number
   - Enter your business email
   - Choose your language (English/BM/Chinese)
3. You're ready to go!

Note: The app can be placed in any folder - it doesn't need to be on the Desktop.

HOW TO ACTIVATE
---------------
1. When you first open the app, you'll see a Hardware ID (HWID)
2. Copy the HWID and send it to the seller via WhatsApp
3. The seller will give you a license key
4. Enter the key and click "Activate"
5. You only need to do this once!

DEMO MODE
---------
If you don't have a license key yet, you can try the demo:
- 7 day trial
- Limited to 10 jobs
- Click "Try Demo" on the activation screen

FEATURES
--------
- Track repair jobs (pending, in-progress, done)
- Customer database with email support
- Appointment scheduling
- Invoice generation (Text or PDF format)
- WhatsApp integration (send invoices directly)
- Email integration (send invoices via email)
- PDF invoice generation (professional format)
- Revenue reports
- Export data to CSV
- Backup and restore
- Network sync (share data between computers)
- Open on Windows startup option
- Supports English, Bahasa Malaysia, and Chinese

EDITING INFORMATION
-------------------
You can edit information after it's been created:
- Jobs: Click "Edit" on any job row
- Customers: Click "Edit" on any customer row
- Appointments: Click "Edit" on any appointment row

PDF INVOICES
------------
To use PDF invoices:
1. Go to Settings
2. Under "Invoice Format", select "PDF"
3. Click Save
4. PDFs are saved in the data/invoices folder

You can also download PDFs directly:
- In Invoices: Click "PDF" button on any unpaid invoice
- In Jobs: Click "PDF" button on completed jobs

TIPS
----
- Add customers first, then create jobs for them
- Click "Done" on a job to mark it complete and create an invoice
- Use "Send WhatsApp" or "Email" to send unpaid invoices to customers
- Check Reports for revenue tracking
- Export data regularly for backup
- Use the search bars in Jobs, Customers, and Invoices to find things quickly

BACKUP YOUR DATA
----------------
Your data is stored in the "data" folder next to the app.
Copy the entire "Kerja Mudah" folder to back up everything.

You can also use the built-in backup:
1. Go to Backup
2. Click "Backup Now"
3. Choose where to save the backup file

TROUBLESHOOTING
---------------
Q: The app won't open
A: Make sure the .exe file is in a folder with write permissions

Q: I reinstalled my computer
A: Contact the seller for a new license key (small fee may apply)

Q: How do I backup?
A: Go to Backup > Backup Now

Q: How do I change language?
A: Go to Settings > Language > Select > Save

Q: Where are my PDF invoices stored?
A: In the data/invoices folder next to the app

Q: Can I move the app to another computer?
A: Yes! Copy the entire folder. You'll need to reactivate with a new license key.
