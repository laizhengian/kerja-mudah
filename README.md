# Kerja Mudah

**Work smarter, not harder.**

Kerja Mudah is a desktop business management app built for small repair and service shops in Malaysia. It runs offline on Windows as a single file — no internet, no monthly fees, no complicated setup.

---

## Who Is This For?

- Phone repair shops
- Car workshops and detailing shops
- Electronics repair services
- Any service-based business that tracks jobs, customers, and invoices

---

## What Does It Do?

### Job Tracking
Create jobs for every repair or service. Track status from pending to in-progress to done. Each job has a unique code, linked customer, item description, problem details, quote, due date, and notes.

### Customer Database
Store all your customers with name, phone, email, and notes. Link customers to jobs and appointments. Search anything instantly.

### Appointment Scheduling
Schedule appointments with date, time, purpose, and notes. See today's appointments on the dashboard.

### Invoice System
Generate invoices from completed jobs. Send them via WhatsApp or Email with one click. Track paid and unpaid invoices. Supports PDF generation with your business details.

### Smart Pickup Reminders
Track uncollected devices. Send automatic WhatsApp reminders to customers who haven't picked up their items.

### Google Review Integration
Add your Google review link in Settings. It's automatically included when you send invoices. More reviews = more customers.

### Revenue Reports
See today's, this week's, and this month's revenue. Track outstanding payments. Know exactly how your business is doing.

### Multi-Language
Switch between English, Bahasa Malaysia, and Chinese anytime in Settings.

### Backup & Restore
One-click backup saves everything. Restore from backup in minutes. Export data to CSV for Excel.

---

## Quick Start

1. Double-click `Kerja Mudah.exe`
2. Enter your business name (required), phone, email, and language
3. Start adding customers and creating jobs
4. Mark jobs as done to create invoices
5. Send invoices via WhatsApp or Email
